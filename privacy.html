Covered: SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Covered is a shift-scheduling assistant operated by Gizra USA, LLC. Restaurants use it to coordinate work shifts with their staff by text message. This policy explains what Covered does with the information involved in those text messages.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number, as entered by the restaurant you work for.
  • Your work schedule: the shifts you're assigned, offered, or cover, and any time off.
  • The text messages you send to Covered and the replies it sends you.

How we use it

Only to schedule work at the restaurant you work for: offering open shifts, sending your schedule, telling you when you're covering a shift, and replying to your texts (for example calling in sick or asking for time off). We don't send marketing or promotional messages.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Your information is visible to the restaurant you work for, for scheduling. To deliver the service we use service providers that process it on our behalf: Twilio, to send and receive text messages, and Anthropic, whose AI model reads incoming texts so that Covered can respond. We don't sell your information.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply START to resume.
  • Reply HELP for help.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Agreeing to receive texts is not a condition of employment.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep scheduling information only as long as the restaurant uses Covered to schedule your work. To ask about or delete your information, contact us below.
