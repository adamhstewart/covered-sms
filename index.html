Shift Text Message Consent

Covered is a scheduling assistant, operated by Gizra, that restaurants use to coordinate work shifts by text message.

What you're agreeing to

I agree to receive text messages from Covered about my work shifts, including open-shift offers and my schedule, on behalf of the restaurant I work for. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

What you'll get

  • Offers of open shifts you can take by replying YES
  • Your schedule when it's published each week
  • Notices when you're covering a coworker's shift
  • Replies when you text Covered, for example to call in sick or ask for time off

Texts are about your shifts only, never marketing. Your number is used only for scheduling at the restaurant you work for, and is never sold or shared. Agreeing is not a condition of employment. You can opt out any time by replying STOP.
